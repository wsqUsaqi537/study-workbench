---
name: pdf-reading
description: Read extracted PDF course material while preserving page references and extraction limits.
---

Use the supplied extracted text and OCR results as evidence; do not invent missing content.
Keep each 【第 N 页】 marker attached to the content from that page and cite only markers present in the input.
Preserve mathematical symbols, operators, and meaningful line or table structure when they are present.
If a reading warning or garbled text appears, state the limitation and do not guess, normalize, or silently repair it.
Do not infer page content from neighboring pages or from the file name.
