Keep each 【第 N 张幻灯片】 marker attached to the slide content and cite only markers present in the input.
Treat text under 【演讲者备注】 as speaker notes for the immediately preceding slide, not as visible slide text.
Preserve equation operators, fraction structure, and subscript or superscript notation as supplied.
If a formula is marked as structurally incomplete or a reading warning is present, do not infer its missing notation; ask the learner to check the original slide.
Do not infer slide order or content from filenames or neighboring slides.
